# 🛒 Supermarket Sales Performance & Customer Behavior Analysis

> **An interactive business intelligence dashboard designed to monitor revenue streams, analyze customer demographics, track product profitability, and evaluate branch performance across supermarket retail operations.**

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

Retail grocery and supermarket management requires continuous oversight of rapid inventory cycles, branch-level profit margins, and changing customer preferences. 

This project delivers an interactive reporting dashboard that translates raw point-of-sale (POS) data into actionable commercial insights. The dashboard monitors top-line performance across \$322.97K in sales and 1,000 transactions, enabling retail managers to optimize product line profitability, evaluate daily revenue against targets, and understand customer purchasing dynamics.

---

## 🔍 Visual Analysis & Business Insights

### 1. Sales Trend & Daily Revenue Volatility
* **Trend Tracker:** A continuous daily line chart tracking revenue from January 2019 through March 2019.
* **Findings:** Highlights sharp peaks and trough cycles, aiding inventory scheduling during high-traffic intervals.

### 2. Product Line Profitability (`Sum of Gross Income`)
Evaluates profit contributions across major retail categories:
* 🥇 **Food and Beverages** – Top contributor ($2,674 income)
* 🥈 **Sports and Travel** – Second highest ($2,625 income)
* 🥉 **Electronic Accessories** – ($2,588 income)
* **Fashion Accessories** – ($2,586 income)
* **Home and Lifestyle** – ($2,565 income)
* **Health and Beauty** – ($2,343 income)

### 3. Customer Demographics (`Sales by Gender`)
* **Female Customers:** **\$167.88K (51.98%)**
* **Male Customers:** **\$155.08K (48.02%)**
* *Takeaway:* Well-balanced customer distribution, with female shoppers driving slightly higher basket sizes and overall spend.

### 4. Branch Benchmarking & Daily Target Tracking
* **Branch Performance:** Branch C leads in overall revenue generation.
* **Goal vs. Actuals:** Daily tracking against dynamic target thresholds (identifying revenue shortfall points to trigger localized promotions).

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters & Slicers

The dashboard provides dynamic multidimensional filtering:
* **Branch:** Drill down into individual store locations (Branch A, B, C).
* **Customer Type:** Compare loyalty/members versus regular walk-in shoppers.
* **Payment Method:** Filter by Cash, Credit Card, or E-wallet transactions.
* **City:** Regional geographic slicing across retail markets.

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

1. **Optimize Inventory for Food & Beverages:** Because it yields the highest gross income ($2,674), maintain strong safety stocks to prevent stockouts during weekend peaks.
2. **Promote Health & Beauty Line:** Health and beauty lagged behind other departments ($2,343); bundle these items with top-performing categories or launch targeted loyalty rewards.
3. **Cross-Branch Best Practices:** Replicate the merchandising and staff incentive models of Branch C across other store locations to raise the baseline across the network.
